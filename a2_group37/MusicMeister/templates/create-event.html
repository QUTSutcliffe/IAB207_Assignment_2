An Acknowledgement to country is an opportunity for anyone to show respect for Traditional Owners and the continuing connection of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to Country. This recognition is important as it contributes to ending the exclusion that has been so damaging to this country. This can be presented by anyone if sincere and informed, unlike a Welcome to Country which is delivered by elders, traditional owners of the land, or Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people. {% for error in form.acknowledgement.errors %}
